A small, focused tool: objectives, key results, weekly check-ins, a simple 0–1 score. No start/target/current-value math, no status field to remember to update — the score you give a key result *is* the color you see.

Built with Phoenix and LiveView, deployed to Fly.io. Currently in private beta, free while we build out more features.
