Tap or scan a card to view a profile and download a vCard. Each user can hold multiple cards and multiple profiles, switching which one is visible on a tap — handy for a conference badge one day and a personal profile the next.

Built solo with Elixir/Phoenix/LiveView over about 3 months of nights and weekends.
