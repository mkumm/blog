No drafting, no weekly lineups — just skin in the game each week. Sprytna Leagues run in two modes:

1. **Classic** — Pick your favorite team for the season. Each week, guess the score you think they'll finish with. Guess under (or exactly) what they scored and you get points equal to your guess — nail it exactly and you double it. Guess over and you get zero for the week. The strategy shifts all season: play it safe with a lead, or go big to catch up.
2. **Multi-team** — The league sets the number of weeks and how many teams each player picks. Same scoring as Classic, just more of it — even a single-week league where everyone picks every team.

Each league has a live score tracker for your picks, plus a projected score per team from [the odds API](https://the-odds-api.com), so you can make confident picks even with minimal football knowledge.

We prototyped this last year with just the two of us and had a blast — excited to see how it plays with more people and more leagues running at once.
