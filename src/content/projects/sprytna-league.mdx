No drafting, no weekly lineups — just skin in the game each week. Pick your favorite team for the season, guess a score, and score points if you guessed under (or exactly) what your team achieved.

Each league has a live score tracker and a projected score for each team, powered by [the odds API](https://the-odds-api.com).
