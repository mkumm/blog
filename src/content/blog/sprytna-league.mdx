This project has been a full year in the making. Sprytna League is the answer to anyone who doesn't want to take time in fantasy drafts, and weekly player selections, etc but still wants a little skin in the game each week. 

Sprytna Leagues run in two modes. 

1. Pick your favorite team for the season. Each week, pick a score you think we get by the end of the match. If you guess fewer points than your team scored, you get points that week equal to your guess. If your guess was over what your team achieved, you get 0 for the week. Guess the number exactly and get double your guess. The goal is to have the highest number of points at the end of the year. The strategy for picking changes a lot as the season progresses - play conservative with the lead? Go crazy to catch up? Lots of fun.

2. The league sets up the number of weeks and how many teams each player gets to select. The player then selects multiple teams and scores for each team. The scoring is the same as the first league type, just a lot more going on. And it's set up where you could have a 1 week league and guess all the teams!

Each league has a live score tracker for your picks, so you can check in and see how close you are for each pick. Scores are provided by [the odds api](https://the-odds-api.com). And to help with your picks, I include a projected score for each team. So even if your football knowledge is pretty minimal, you can still make your picks with confidence.

We prototyped this game last year and had a lot of fun with just the two of us. I can't wait to see how it plays out with multiple people and multiple leagues going on!
